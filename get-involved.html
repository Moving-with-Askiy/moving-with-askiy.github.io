An Open Call

Why your support matters

Cost, transportation, gear, experience, and confidence all stand between Indigenous peoples and the land that has always held them. We want every program we run to be free and welcoming, grounded in Indigenous ways of being and doing. We can't do that alone.

1

Programs at no cost

Sponsorship covers program fees, permits, and park passes so cost is never the reason someone stays home.

2

Getting people there

Rides from the city and from communities to trailheads, crags, rivers, and ski hills.

3

Gear for everyone

Helmets, harnesses, packs, skis, boots, and outerwear, so equipment is never a barrier.

4

Honouring knowledge

Honoraria and protocol for the Elders and Knowledge Keepers who guide how we move on the land.
