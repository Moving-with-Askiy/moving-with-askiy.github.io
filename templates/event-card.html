Event card

A reusable card for the Events section of Events & Programs: an optional photo carousel, a title, and a short description.

How to add an event

  1. Put the event's photos in images/events/. Resize them to about 1600px wide first so the page stays fast.
  2. Copy the card below into the <div class="event-grid"> in the Events section of programs.html. Newest events go first.
  3. Change the title, the description, and the aria-label on the carousel ("Photos from …").
  4. Add one <figure class="event-card__slide"> per photo, each with a short alt description of what's in the photo.
  5. No photos? Delete the whole event-card__carousel block. One photo? Keep a single slide; the arrows and dots only appear when there are two or more.

Photos fill a 4:3 frame. If a tall or wide photo cuts off the wrong part, add style="object-position: center 30%" to its <img> (the second number moves the frame up or down).

Copy this

<article class="event-card">
  <!-- Optional: delete this whole block if the event has no photos -->
  <div class="event-card__carousel" data-carousel aria-label="Photos from EVENT TITLE">
    <div class="event-card__track">
      <figure class="event-card__slide">
        <img src="images/events/PHOTO-1.jpg" alt="DESCRIBE PHOTO 1" loading="lazy" decoding="async">
      </figure>
      <figure class="event-card__slide">
        <img src="images/events/PHOTO-2.jpg" alt="DESCRIBE PHOTO 2" loading="lazy" decoding="async">
      </figure>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="event-card__body">
    <h3 class="event-card__title">EVENT TITLE</h3>
    <p class="event-card__description">A sentence or two about the event.</p>
  </div>
</article>
